# Finora App FAQ

## How do I add a transaction or expense?
To add a transaction in Finora, tap the "+" button on the Expenses tab. Fill in the amount, select whether it is an income or expense, choose a category (like Food, Rent, Transport, Shopping, Entertainment, Health, Utilities, Education, Salary, Freelance, Investment, Travel, or Other), add an optional description, and pick the date. Tap Save and your transaction will be recorded instantly. You can also scan a receipt to automatically extract the amount.

## How do I set my monthly budget?
Go to your Profile tab in Finora and look for the Monthly Budget field. Enter the amount you want to set as your spending limit for the month. The AI Coach will automatically track your spending against this budget and alert you if you are close to exceeding it or have already gone over.

## How do I create a savings goal?
Navigate to the Goals tab and tap "Add Goal." Give your goal a name (like "Emergency Fund" or "New Car"), set a target amount, pick a category (Savings, Investment, Emergency, Travel, Vacation, Education, Home, Car, Retirement, Debt Payoff, or Other), and optionally set a deadline. You can then make deposits toward your goal over time. Finora will track your progress percentage automatically.

## How do I track my investments?
Go to the Invest tab to view your portfolio. Tap "Add Investment" to log a new holding. Search for the asset by name or symbol, enter the quantity you own and your average buy price. Finora will fetch current market prices and calculate your unrealized profit/loss, return percentage, and total portfolio value.

## What is the Financial Health Score?
The Financial Health Score is a number from 0 to 100 that Finora calculates based on your savings rate (income minus expenses divided by income), whether you are within your monthly budget, and how many financial goals you have completed. A score above 75 is excellent, 40-75 means you are doing okay but can improve, and below 40 means you need to take action to get your finances on track.

## How does the AI Coach work?
The AI Coach is your personal financial assistant inside Finora. You can ask it about your balance, spending breakdown, budget status, goal progress, portfolio performance, and general financial education topics like investing, budgeting strategies, debt management, and retirement planning. It uses your real financial data to give you personalized answers.

## What categories are available for expenses?
Finora supports these expense categories: Food, Rent, Transport, Shopping, Entertainment, Health, Utilities, Education, Salary, Freelance, Investment, Travel, and Other. Each transaction must be assigned to one category for accurate spending analysis.

## How do I view my spending analytics?
Go to the Expenses tab to see your transaction history. The analytics section shows your total spending for the month, a breakdown by category, and your recent transactions. The AI Coach can also give you a detailed spending analysis if you ask "show my spending" or "where did my money go."

## Can I edit or delete a transaction?
Yes. In the Expenses tab, tap on any transaction to open it. You can then edit the amount, category, description, or date. You can also delete the transaction entirely. Changes are reflected immediately in your balance and analytics.

## What types of investments can I track?
Finora supports tracking Stocks, Cryptocurrency, Forex, ETFs, Commodities, NFTs, Bonds, Mutual Funds, and Real Estate. Each asset type has dedicated fields for quantity, buy price, current price, and unrealized P&L.

## How do goal deposits work?
When you make progress toward a goal, go to the goal detail screen and add a deposit. Enter the amount you are contributing. Finora automatically updates your current progress and percentage. When your deposits reach or exceed the target amount, the goal is automatically marked as completed.

## Is my financial data safe?
Finora stores your data securely on our backend servers. All API communication is authenticated using JWT tokens, which means only you can access your financial data. Your password is encrypted and never stored in plain text.
